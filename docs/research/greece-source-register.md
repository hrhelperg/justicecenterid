contain the facts attributed to it, and independently re-checked in an adversarial verification
pass. Articles 93§4 and 105 of the Constitution were additionally re-confirmed by hand at authoring
time. Institution names are given in Greek with a transliteration and an English rendering.

two honest substitutions were made and are flagged wherever the facts they support appear.

## Two honest limits (flagged wherever they bear on the text)

1. **Bot-walled Greek official sites.** The Hellenic Parliament's official English Constitution, the
   Hellenic Police (`astynomia.gr`), and the Ministry of Citizen Protection (`minocp.gov.gr`) all
   returned HTTP 403. So the Constitution is quoted from the **unofficial Constitute translation**
   (cross-checked against the current e-Justice portal), and the ministerial placement of the police
   and prisons from the **U.S. State Department** report. Both substitutions are named on the pages.
2. **Not fetched from primary text.** The **Code of Criminal Procedure** article numbers for the
   police as preliminary investigators, and the **instrument/date** transferring corrections to the
   Ministry of Citizen Protection, were not fetched; the prosecutor-led, examining-magistrate model
   is established from the Constitution and e-Justice, and no CPC section or transfer date is
   asserted.

## Naming

The three supreme courts are named in Greek + transliteration + English: **Άρειος Πάγος** (Áreios
Págos, "Supreme Civil and Criminal Court"), **Συμβούλιο της Επικρατείας** (Symvoúlio tis Epikrateías,
"Council of State" / Supreme Administrative Court), **Ελεγκτικό Συνέδριο** (Elegktikó Synédrio,
"Court of Audit"). The conflict-resolving special court is the **Ανώτατο Ειδικό Δικαστήριο** (Special
Highest Court).
