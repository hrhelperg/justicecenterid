# Czechia — research plan

Czechia is country 4 of Batch B. It is a clean unitary case, so the research focused on three
things that distinguish it: the court structure, where the prosecution sits, and the absence of a
judicial council.

## Questions the pilot had to answer

1. **Unitary or devolved?** — Constitution 1993: unitary, with self-governing regions and
   municipalities but no justice competence at regional level. The regional courts and the fourteen
   regional police directorates are national organs named after regions. → one `cz` record, all
   functions `own`, no region record.
2. **How many apex courts?** — three: the Supreme Court (Art. 92), a separate Supreme Administrative
   Court (Art. 91(1)), and a separate Constitutional Court (Arts. 83–84, fifteen Justices for
   ten-year terms) outside the ordinary hierarchy. Tri-apex, same family as Austria.
3. **Where does the prosecution sit?** — the Public Prosecutor's Office is "a component of the
   executive power … part of the Ministry of Justice" (its own account; Constitution Art. 80). The
   most executive-attached prosecution in Batch B.
4. **Any reform?** — yes, and enacted: an amendment to Act No. 283/1993 adopted 7 March 2024, in
   force 1 July 2024, giving the Prosecutor General a seven-year non-renewable term and making chief
   prosecutors dismissible only by disciplinary proceedings (EC 2024 Rule of Law Report). Recorded
   as enacted law with dates, attributed.
5. **Is there a judicial council?** — no national one. Court administration is by the Ministry of
   Justice; only advisory councils exist within courts. Stated as absence, not implied to exist.
6. **Police, prisons, oversight?** — Policie ČR (national, Interior Ministry, Act 273/2008);
   Vězeňská služba (Ministry of Justice, Act 555/1992, plus the Judicial Guard); Ombudsman + GIBS
   (Act 341/2011).
7. **Prison numbers?** — SPACE I 2024: 19,569 / 20,301 / density 96.4 (under capacity), as a limited
   restricted claim.

## Sources targeted

Constitutional Court (Constitution EN), EU e-Justice, the prosecution/police/prison/ombudsman/GIBS
official sites, the EC 2024 Rule of Law Report, and the shared CoE SPACE I 2024.

## Deliberate limit

The exact Criminal Procedure Code section for pre-trial prosecutorial supervision was not fetched
from primary text; the role is anchored to the Constitution and the prosecution's account, and no
section number is asserted. Forensics, border/customs, history and a timeline are deferred.

## Model discipline

No new schema. Czechia reuses unitary/national values and the multi-apex-court prose pattern; the
prosecution-in-the-ministry and no-judicial-council facts live in prose and in the tests.
