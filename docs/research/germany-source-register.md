# Germany source register

**Research date:** all sources read and confirmed **2026-07-24**.
**Registry:** `src/content/sources.ts`, ids prefixed `de-` plus `coe-space-i-2024`.

## Verification standard

`content-confirmed` means the document was retrieved and read and confirmed to contain the text
it is cited for. No source here is marked verified on an HTTP status.

Two situations recur and are not equivalent:

- **Bot-walled but readable.** `legifrance.gouv.fr` behaved this way in the France pilot. German
  official sources did not block retrieval, but the principle stands: a non-200 from a
  known-good official host means "needs human re-check", never "dead source".
- **Reachable but truncated.** The official English translations of the StPO and GVG are single
  very long pages; retrieval returned only their opening sections and never reached §§ 141–147
  GVG or §§ 152/160/163 StPO. That is why those provisions are cited from the **authoritative
  German text** instead — which is the better citation regardless.

the code provisions and why the Basic Law's stated amendment date is recorded on its source.

## Outstanding

- No source supports the **statutory competences** of the BKA or the Bundespolizei.
- No source supports **any Land's** institutional organisation.
- No source supports German forensic, border/customs or oversight institutions — the four
  deferred modules.
- The German-language per-section pages display no _Stand_ date, so currency rests on the
  publisher (BMJ/BfJ) rather than a printed version date. Recorded rather than glossed.
