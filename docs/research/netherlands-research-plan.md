# Netherlands — research plan

## Jurisdiction scoping

Decentralised unitary state (not federal). The four justice functions are national competence;
provinces and municipalities administer none of them. One country-level jurisdiction record.
Scope is the European country of the Netherlands, NOT the wider Kingdom (Aruba, Curaçao, Sint
Maarten run their own justice systems; the Hoge Raad is the shared court of cassation).

## Research questions

- Constitutional basis of the justice system (Grondwet Chapter 6).
- Is there a constitutional court / judicial review of statutes? (Answer: no — Art. 120.)
- Is the prosecution independent of the executive? (Answer: no — OM is in the judiciary but under
  the Minister of Justice and Security.)
- Court hierarchy and apex (ordinary + administrative).
- Police structure and who directs criminal investigation.
- Prison authority.
- Oversight bodies.
- Any enacted-with-date institutional reform.

## Model stress test

Pure reuse: unitary, all functions `own` at the country level. No new level, scope, or authority
basis. Two attribute facts (OM-under-minister; no judicial review of statutes) are prose, not
schema. See netherlands-model-findings.md.

## Source hierarchy

Constitution (official English translation) → the OM's own site, Government of the Netherlands,
DJI, the judiciary's portal → EU e-Justice Portal (intergovernmental corroboration) → Council of
Europe SPACE I for prison figures. Official consolidated legislation (wetten.overheid.nl) was
unreachable; statute text is not quoted.
