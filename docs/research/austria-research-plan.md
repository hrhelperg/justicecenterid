# Austria — research plan

Austria is country 3 of Batch B. It carried a specific model question forward from the batch
research: **is Austria's justice federal (like Belgium) or devolved to the Länder (like Germany)?**
The answer governs whether Austria gets Land records.

## Questions the pilot had to answer

1. **Federal or devolved for justice?** — B-VG Art. 10(1) Z 6 (justice, criminal/civil law,
   protective institutions = federal), Z 7 (security police = federal save local), and Art. 82(1)
   ("ordinary jurisdiction emanates from the Federation") → justice is **federal**. Belgium-type,
   inverse of Germany. → one `federal` record, all core competences `exclusive-federal`, **no** Land
   record. The only devolved element is each Land organising its own administrative court
   (Art. 129) — noted in prose, not a Land record.
2. **How many apex courts?** — three co-equal: OGH (civil/criminal, Art. 92), VfGH (constitutional,
   Art. 144), VwGH (administrative, Art. 133) — confirmed by the OGH's own statement that the three
   are "on the same level".
3. **What is the status of the prosecution?** — Art. 90a: organs of the ordinary judiciary,
   instruction-bound; StAG §2(1): subordinate up a chain terminating at the **Federal Minister of
   Justice**. So the apex is currently the executive — a distinct answer from Portugal (autonomous)
   and Italy (inside the judiciary).
4. **Is there a pending reform?** — yes: a 2026 government consultation draft to create an
   independent Bundesstaatsanwaltschaft, replacing the Minister at the apex. **Not enacted** —
   recorded neutrally in prose as a draft.
5. **Police and investigation?** — a single national police under the Interior Minister
   (Arts. 78a/78b), nine federal directorates; the prosecutor leads the investigation, the criminal
   police obey (StPO §101, §99).
6. **Prisons and numbers?** — the federal Ministry of Justice runs the Justizanstalten. SPACE I 2024
   reported 9,258 inmates and a rate of 101.1/100k but **no capacity** — so the restricted claim
   states population + rate and makes **no** density claim.

## Sources targeted (all primary or official)

RIS (B-VG, StAG, StPO), the OGH, the Ministries of Justice and the Interior, the Volksanwaltschaft,
the government consultation portal, and the shared CoE SPACE I 2024. The EU e-Justice Austria page
could not be fetched and nothing depends on it.

## What was deliberately left out

Forensics and border/customs (safety-sensitive, not researched to standard), and institutional
history and a timeline (need scholarly and per-milestone sources). All four are deferred with
reasons and produce no routes.

## Model discipline

No new schema. Austria reuses the `federal` level and `exclusive-federal` legislative-competence
values already minted for Belgium; the three-apex-courts and prosecution-under-the-Minister facts
live in prose and in the tests.
