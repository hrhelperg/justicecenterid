# Belgium — source register

Every source used for the Belgium pages, verified on 2026-07-26 and independently re-checked in
an adversarial fact-check pass. Two provenance corrections from that pass were applied (see below).

## Access note

Two official sites could not be reached: the **Federal Police's own website** (unreachable / 403)
and the **belgium.be** portal (bot-walled). The Federal Police's internal directorates and the
specific federal prison directorate are therefore **not asserted**. The Constitution was read from
the House of Representatives' **official English PDF**; the integrated-police structure from the
**consolidated Law of 7 December 1998 on the official Justel database**; the courts and prosecution
from the **Court of Cassation's own site** and the **EU e-Justice Portal**.

## Provenance corrections applied (from the verification pass)

1. The official English translation of **Constitution Art. 147** reads "There is a **Supreme Court**
   for all Belgium." The institution is the **Court of Cassation** — so its name is taken from the
   court's own site, and "Supreme Court" is presented as the official translation, not as a
   differently-named body.
2. The direction of criminal investigation is **paraphrased** from the official sources, not quoted
   from a search summary.

## Deliberate gaps (stated, not filled)

- Federal Police internal directorates (DGA/DGJ/DGR) — official site unreachable.
- The specific federal prison directorate (understood to be the Directorate-General of Penitentiary
  Institutions, DG EPI) — not confirmed from a reachable official page; only the federal character
  of the prison system is asserted.
- Police-oversight committee (Comité P) and the federal ombudsman — named in general terms, not
  confirmed from a primary source.
- Current allocation of youth-justice competence between the federal level and the Communities —
  not researched.
- Primary text of the Judicial Code and the FPS Justice royal orders — not fetched; the "5 appeal
  courts / 12 districts" counts rest on the EU e-Justice Portal.
