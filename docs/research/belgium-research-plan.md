# Belgium — research plan

## Jurisdiction scoping

Federal state (Constitution art. 1) — but the model question is whether the justice functions are
devolved. **Answer: they are federal.** Single national bodies "for all Belgium" (Constitutional
Court art. 142, Court of Cassation art. 147, High Council of Justice + prosecution art. 151),
federal prisons, and a federal-plus-local integrated police. So one `federal` jurisdiction record,
all functions `own`; no Community/Region record (none does justice work).

## Research questions

- Is justice a federal or a devolved competence? (Federal.)
- Apex court(s): the three-court structure (Cassation / Constitutional Court / Council of State).
- Prosecution independence: Art. 151 §1 (independent in individual cases, but the minister may
  order prosecutions and set criminal policy).
- Police structure: integrated two-level (federal + local), Law of 7 December 1998.
- Prison authority (federal).
- Oversight (High Council of Justice).

## Model stress test

Reuse, no new schema — Belgium is the _inverse_ of Germany: federal but justice-centralised,
carried by the scope values (all `own` + `exclusive-federal`) rather than a new field. See
belgium-model-findings.md.
