Research date: **2026-07-27**. Every source below was read or retrieved on that date, confirmed to
contain the facts attributed to it, and independently re-checked in an adversarial verification
pass. Sections 23/24 of the Constitution Act, section 16 of the Policing Act, and the World Prison
Brief figures were re-confirmed by hand at authoring time. Institution names are given with their
official Māori forms.

## Access notes and honest limits

- **legislation.govt.nz** (the official statute portal) sits behind an **AWS WAF JavaScript
  challenge**, so the Constitution Act and Policing Act were read from the UN FAO mirror and a
  police-human-rights mirror; the load-bearing sections were re-confirmed verbatim.
- **Department of Corrections, the Ombudsman, and the Human Rights Commission** sites returned HTTP
  403; the administering department is taken from the World Prison Brief, and the OPCAT/torture-
  prevention and corrections-statute details rest on secondary reading (flagged in the dossier).
- The **prosecutor-independence** verbatim wording is in the Solicitor-General's Prosecution
  Guidelines, not re-fetched; the independence is described from the Law-Officer structure.
- **World Prison Brief capacity is from 2019** while the population is from 2026, so the corrections
  page states population + rate only and makes **no** current occupancy/density claim.
