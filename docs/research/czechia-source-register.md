# Czechia — source register

Research date: **2026-07-26**. Every source below was read or retrieved on that date, confirmed to
contain the facts attributed to it, and independently re-checked in an adversarial verification
pass. Institution names are given in Czech and English.

**Translation note.** The Constitutional Court's official English text renders "státní
zastupitelství" as **State Attorney's Office** (Art. 80); the service brands in English as the
**Public Prosecutor's Office**, headed by the **Prosecutor General's Office** (Nejvyšší státní
zastupitelství). Both denote the same institution.

## The one flagged limit

The **exact Criminal Procedure Code section** (search-surfaced as § 174 of Act No. 141/1961) for
prosecutorial supervision of pre-trial proceedings was **not** fetched from primary statutory text.
The _substance_ — that the prosecution directs and supervises the pre-trial phase — is established by
Constitution Art. 80 and the prosecution's own account, so the investigations page anchors the role
there and does **not** assert a section number. This is stated in the dossier's uncertainty and in
the sources module.

## Access notes

- The Constitution and the Commission report were fetched as PDFs and text-extracted; the
  institutional pages were read directly.
- The GIBS statutory basis (Act No. 341/2011) was flagged as search-only in research and
  **confirmed** in verification.
