# France oversight — function matrix

**Built 2026-08-10, Wave 6.** Rows are the three bodies named in the brief. Cells are
**YES / NO / CONDITIONAL / NOT ESTABLISHED**, decided from the French instruments listed in
`docs/research/france-police-oversight-deep-dive.md` §1.

`NOT ESTABLISHED` never means `NO`. It means the instruments read do not settle it.

## Why each non-obvious cell reads as it does

**IGPN — INTERNAL.** Décret n° 2013-784 art. 1: « L'inspection générale de la police nationale
est un service actif de la direction générale de la police nationale. » A service of the force
it examines.

**IGGN — INTERNAL.** Code de la défense art. D3122-12: « Le directeur général de la gendarmerie
nationale dispose de l'inspection générale de la gendarmerie nationale. »

**Défenseur des droits — GENERAL, not police-specific.** Article 4 of the loi organique gives
five mandates; security deontology is one, and it covers « les personnes exerçant des activités
de sécurité », which the institution's own competence statement extends to municipal police,
prison staff, customs officers, public-transport surveillance agents, stewarding services and
**private security employees**. A mandate that reaches private guards is not a police mandate.

**Défenseur des droits — INSPECTION: CONDITIONAL.** He may carry out « vérifications sur place »
in the premises of the persons concerned (art. 22), but tied to a matter before him. There is no
standing programme of inspection of an organisation, which is what the inspection cell means for
the two inspections générales. Different function, same English word.

**IGPN — COMPLAINT INTAKE: CONDITIONAL.** Two channels that must not be merged. Décret art. 4:
« Elle reçoit les plaintes et dénonciations. » But the public-facing _plateforme de signalement_
states: « Il ne s'agit ni d'un service d'urgence, ni d'un service d'enquête, ni d'un service de
plainte. » Intake exists; a general public-complaint mechanism in the sense the English phrase
implies does not.

**IGGN — COMPLAINT INTAKE: YES.** « assure le recueil et le traitement des doléances que lui
adressent les particuliers » — _doléances_, in terms, from private individuals.

**IGPN — ADMINISTRATIVE INVESTIGATION: CONDITIONAL.** Art. 5 (as modified 16 July 2025) requires
instruction from the Minister, the DGPN, the DGSI or the préfet de police, with a narrow urgency
exception. It cannot simply open one.

**IGGN — ADMINISTRATIVE INVESTIGATION: YES.** « diligente, sur instruction ou d'initiative, les
enquêtes administratives ». Initiative is express.

**IGPN — CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION: YES.** Art. 4: « diligente des enquêtes judiciaires, d'initiative
ou sur instruction de l'autorité judiciaire ».

**IGGN — CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION: CONDITIONAL.** « elle diligente les enquêtes judiciaires qui lui
sont confiées et satisfait aux réquisitions judiciaires » — entrusted, not self-opened.

**Défenseur des droits — CRIMINAL INVESTIGATION: NO.** The organic law confers investigative
powers over réclamations, not criminal investigation; where facts appear to constitute a crime or
délit he informs the procureur de la République instead.

**IGPN — DISCIPLINARY RECOMMENDATION: NOT ESTABLISHED.** The instruments read confer inspection,
audit, advice and investigation. They do not say the IGPN recommends a disciplinary outcome; the
administrative investigation is conducted for the authority that ordered it.

**IGGN — DISCIPLINARY RECOMMENDATION: CONDITIONAL.** It makes « toutes recommandations
nécessaires » about deontology **rules**. That is rule-level, not a recommendation on an
individual case, and the distinction is not collapsed here.

**Défenseur des droits — DISCIPLINARY RECOMMENDATION: YES / DISCIPLINARY DECISION: NO.** Art. 29:
he may « saisir l'autorité investie du pouvoir d'engager les poursuites disciplinaires ». The
same sentence that grants the referral locates the decision elsewhere, which is why this is the
one `NO` in the disciplinary-decision row rather than a `NOT ESTABLISHED`.

**IGPN — PROSECUTORIAL REFERRAL: NOT ESTABLISHED.** The IGPN's relationship to the procureur in
the instruments read is one of **direction** — it investigates on judicial instruction — rather
than of referral. No distinct duty to report is established in those texts. This is an absence of
evidence, and the row says so.

**IGGN — PROSECUTORIAL REFERRAL: YES.** It « informe l'autorité judiciaire lorsque les manquements
constatés sont susceptibles de constituer une infraction pénale ».

**IGPN — RIGHTS / ETHICS REVIEW: NOT ESTABLISHED.** The IGGN's arrêté says it is « chargée du
respect des règles de déontologie » in terms. The IGPN's décret and arrêté, as read, do not. Both
forces are subject to one deontology code, so this may well be an artefact of which instruments
were read — which is precisely why the cell is NOT ESTABLISHED and not NO.

**LEGAL INDEPENDENCE.** For the two inspections, `NO` is not a criticism; it is what the
attachment provisions say. For the Défenseur, `YES` rests on text: « ne reçoit et ne sollicite
… aucune instruction », immunity for opinions and acts, and a six-year non-renewable mandate
under Constitution art. 71-1.

**PUBLIC ACCESS — YES for all three, meaning three different things.** An IGPN _signalement_
that is expressly not a complaint; IGGN _doléances_ from individuals; free _saisine_ of the
Défenseur by any victim or witness. Same cell value, three non-interchangeable routes.

## What the shape of this matrix means

Read down the columns rather than across the rows.

The two **internal** bodies hold the investigative powers — one of them can open a criminal
investigation on its own initiative. The two **external** bodies hold none: they verify,
recommend, enjoin, publish and refer.

So in France, position and power run in **opposite** directions. That is the single most
important structural fact this wave established, and it is the reason `oversightPosture`
records position and stops there.
