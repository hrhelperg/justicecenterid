# Denmark — research plan

## Jurisdiction scoping

Unitary constitutional monarchy; all four justice functions national, under the Ministry of
Justice. One country-level record, all `own`. Scope is the country of Denmark; the Faroe Islands
and Greenland self-govern (statutory Home Rule/Self-Government, not federalism) with their own
court and police districts — noted in prose, not modelled.

## Research questions

- Constitutional basis (1953 Grundlov): judicial independence (§62), one court system (§63).
- Apex court and whether there is a constitutional/administrative court (there is not — §63).
- Prosecution independence (under the Ministry of Justice — not independent).
- The integrated police-prosecution model (Police Commissioner = police head + local prosecutor).
- Police structure (Rigspolitiet, 12 districts + NSK 2022).
- Prison authority (Kriminalforsorgen, under the Ministry of Justice).
- Oversight (Parliamentary Ombudsman; Independent Police Complaints Authority; Court Administration).

## Model stress test

Reuse, no new schema. Two features are prose, not schema: the integrated police-prosecution
(institutional composition) and the ordinary-courts-do-everything structure. Prosecution
"under the ministry" is an attribute in prose. See denmark-model-findings.md.
