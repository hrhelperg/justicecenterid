# United States source register

**Research date:** all sources read and confirmed **2026-07-24**.
**Registry:** `src/content/sources.ts`, ids prefixed `us-`.

## Verification standard

`content-confirmed` means the document was retrieved and read and confirmed to contain the text
it is cited for. No source is marked verified on an HTTP status.

Several official hosts — justice.gov, fbi.gov, and some others — return **HTTP 403** to an
automated request while serving their pages to a browser, the same bot-wall pattern documented
for legifrance.gouv.fr in the France pilot. Where the _enacted statutory text_ was needed, it is
cited from the **United States Code as reproduced verbatim by the Legal Information Institute**
(Cornell Law School). The enacted text is authoritative regardless of which publisher reproduces
it; the publisher field says where it was read.

## Sources deliberately not used

| Candidate                                                          | Why not                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| justice.gov, fbi.gov agency pages                                  | Bot-walled (403). The enacted statutory basis was cited from the U.S. Code via Cornell LII instead; agency mission prose was not needed beyond what the Marshals page (which did resolve) supports. |
| Any specific named tribe's constitution or courts                  | Not researched. Tribal jurisdiction is modelled as a category from the BIA PL 280 source; no specific nation is described.                                                                          |
| Any individual state's constitution, courts, police or corrections | Not researched. The two state records (California, Louisiana) assert only the structural point each tests.                                                                                          |
| BJS Jail Inmates series                                            | Named on the corrections page to draw the prison/jail distinction, but no jail figure is published, so it is not cited as a claim source.                                                           |

## Outstanding

- No source supports the statutory competences of individual federal agencies (FBI, DEA, ATF)
  beyond the general § 533 basis.
- No source supports any individual state, county, parish or municipal institution.
- The detailed allocation of criminal jurisdiction in Indian country (the Major Crimes Act,
  state-specific PL 280 effects, recent Supreme Court decisions) is not researched.
- DC's distinctive court and prosecution arrangements are not researched; those scopes are
  recorded as `unknown`.
