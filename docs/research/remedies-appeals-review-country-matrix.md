# Remedies, appeals and review — country matrix

Compiled 2026-08-26 for Wave 19. Every cell rests on a source cited in
`src/content/sources.ts` and verified for this wave or an earlier one.

**Values.** `YES` — established by a cited source. `NO` — a cited source establishes the
negative. `CONDITIONAL` — available on stated conditions. **`NOT ESTABLISHED`** — this platform
has not obtained evidence either way. `NOT ESTABLISHED` is never a synonym for `NO`, and no cell
was filled by inference from a neighbouring system.

## 4. Unlawfully obtained evidence

| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |

Three constitutions, two answers. The claim "unlawfully obtained evidence is always excluded" is
false of two of the three, and the claim "exclusion is always discretionary" is not supported for
the third.

error this row exists to prevent. Two systems here protect the same interest without the name.

## 7. What this matrix does not establish

- No cell records how often a mechanism is used, how long it takes, or whether it works.
- No system is ranked, and no "best" or "strongest" judgment is made or implied.
- `NOT ESTABLISHED` appears 19 times. Each is a gap in this platform's sourcing, not a finding
  about the system.
