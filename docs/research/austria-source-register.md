# Austria — source register

Research date: **2026-07-26**. Every source below was read or retrieved on that date, confirmed to
contain the facts attributed to it, and independently re-checked in an adversarial verification
pass. Four load-bearing passages were additionally re-fetched by hand at authoring time. This
register records each source's exact scope and where it ends.

Everything is anchored to Austrian primary law (the Federation's official legal-information system,
RIS) and official institutions. There is no single official English translation of the Constitution,
so the German text is authoritative; institution names are given in German with an English gloss.

## Access notes

- **RIS renders statute text in raw HTML**, so the constitutional articles and both statutes were
  retrieved directly via `curl`. Four load-bearing passages were re-fetched by hand at authoring
  time and matched verbatim: **B-VG Art. 82(1)** ("Die ordentliche Gerichtsbarkeit geht vom Bund
  aus"), **B-VG Art. 90a** ("Staatsanwälte sind Organe der ordentlichen Gerichtsbarkeit"), **StAG
  §2(1)** ("dem Bundesminister für Justiz unmittelbar untergeordnet und weisungsgebunden"), and the
  **OGH's** "three Courts are hierarchically on the same level" statement.
- **The EU e-Justice Austria page could not be fetched** (the portal was restructured). No fact
  depends on it — everything is anchored to Austrian primary law and official institutions.

## Honest handling of the prison figure

Austria is the one Batch B country for which SPACE I reported **no capacity**. The corrections page
therefore states the population count and the rate per 100,000 and makes **no** density or
overcrowding claim of any kind — inventing an occupancy level would go beyond the source. This is
recorded in the restricted claim's `limitation` and asserted by a test.
