# Ireland research plan

## Objective

Test the model against a unitary common-law state, and against the discipline the earlier
pilots did not need: scope integrity. Publishing Ireland was the means; establishing whether the
model needed to change (it did not) was the goal. Results:
[ireland-model-findings.md](./ireland-model-findings.md).

## Why Ireland, after three federal-ish systems

France (unitary civil-law), Germany (symmetric federal), the US (decentralised federal with
multiple sovereignties) all stretched the _structural_ model. Ireland changes the _legal
tradition_ — common law — in a _unitary_ state, and adds a hazard the others did not carry:
"Ireland" is routinely conflated with the island of Ireland and with Northern Ireland, which is
a separate United Kingdom jurisdiction.

## Scope decision

Thirteen candidate modules; nine published, four deferred. The gate applied per module: can
every claim be traced to a source read or reader-accessibly retrieved, AND can it be stated
while holding scope integrity — Ireland the State, not the island, not Northern Ireland?

Oversight is published (it is in Ireland's minimum expected set and, unusually, is a strength of
this pilot because the 2 April 2025 reform is a clean current/historical test). Border-and-customs
is deferred precisely because it is where scope integrity is most fragile.

## Source access — the defining practical constraint

Irish official sites WAF-block automated requests almost universally. The approach, per the
master spec's allowance for reader-accessible blocked sources:

- **Direct reads** where possible: the DPP's own site, the Police Ombudsman's own site, and the
  SPACE I report PDF. Verbatim quotes come only from these.
- **Search retrieval of the exact official page** for the WAF-blocked ones (Courts Service, Irish
  Statute Book, gov.ie, Citizens Information), cross-corroborated across sources and stated as
  sourced facts, never as verbatim quotation.

The source register records the access path for every source. No constitutional article is
quoted verbatim, because the primary text was blocked; provisions are described through official
secondary sources.

## Sample strategy

Ireland is unitary, so there is no sub-national sample to build — one country-level jurisdiction
record states the whole arrangement. This is the correct handling, not a shortfall: France
established that a unitary state needs one record.

## What was researched but not published

| Module             | Blocker                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forensics          | Forensic Science Ireland and the State Laboratory arrangements not read; safety-sensitive section.                                                                                                                                           |
| Border and customs | The Common Travel Area, the EU customs/Schengen frame and the land border with Northern Ireland must be distinguished with great care; this is the highest scope-integrity risk in the dossier and could not be researched to that standard. |
| History            | The foundation of the State (1922), the Civil War and the constitutional settlement, inseparable from the relationship with the United Kingdom; needs archival sources and the same scope care.                                              |
| Timeline           | Several dated facts exist but a responsible timeline needs a primary source per milestone, which the WAF blocking prevented.                                                                                                                 |

## Rules held throughout

- **Scope integrity on every page:** Ireland the State, explicitly not the island and not
  Northern Ireland; nothing describes Northern Ireland.
- **No forced Anglo-American equivalents:** An Garda Síochána is named in Irish and as a police
  AND security service; the DPP is not equated with a British or American prosecutor; the
  written constitution with judicial review is distinguished from the UK arrangement.
- **The Special Criminal Court** is given its emergency-provision and independent-review context,
  not presented as an ordinary court.
- **Oversight recency:** current bodies named, historical bodies marked with the 2 April 2025
  date.
- No operational content; no legal advice; no crime sensationalism; no ranking of Ireland against
  any other country.
