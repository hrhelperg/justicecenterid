# Australia research plan

## Objective

Independently test whether the Canada pilot's `contracted` FunctionScope survives a second
federation — the AFP delivering ACT community policing — or was overfitted to the RCMP. The
answer (it survives, earned by repetition) and the Australia/Canada contrast are the findings:
[australia-model-findings.md](./australia-model-findings.md).

## Why Australia, right after Canada

Australia has the same contract-policing shape (a jurisdiction responsible for policing but
having a federal institution deliver it) in a constitutionally different federation, so it is the
natural adversarial test of `contracted`. It is also the sharp contrast to Canada on the axis of
where power comes from: Australia's residual power is reserved to the states (Constitution s.107,
the US model), and there is no national criminal code — each state and territory writes its own
criminal law.

## The central test

Does the ACT/AFP arrangement fit `contracted`? The research established: the ACT runs no police
of its own; the AFP delivers ACT community policing "on behalf of the ACT Government" (AFP Act
1979 s.8(1)(a)/(1A); a Policing Arrangement and Purchase Agreement); the Northern Territory, a
self-governing territory like the ACT, runs its OWN police force. A critic tasked to refute the
value concluded **survives-with-refinement**: the ACT is `contracted`, the NT is `own`, and the
value is validated by repetition. The refinement — `contracted` is agnostic about which order's
law creates the arrangement (Canada: holder-side agreement; Australia: the provider's Commonwealth
statute) — is documentary, kept in prose; no schema change.

## No new schema

The critic's proposed "source-of-power" attribute is exactly the existing `authorityBasis` field:
Australian states are `reserved-powers` (s.107) with `legalSystemScope: own` (own criminal law) —
the US pattern; the ACT and NT are `federal-plenary` (self-governing under Commonwealth statute —
the DC/Yukon value). So Australia reuses `contracted`, `reserved-powers` and `federal-plenary`,
and forces nothing new.

## Jurisdiction samples (4)

au (Commonwealth), au-nsw (a large reserved-power state, own police), au-act (contracted policing),
au-nt (own policing). The ACT/NT pair is the whole point: two self-governing territories that
differ only in whether they procure policing from the Commonwealth. No public sub-national pages.

## Sources and access

The Constitution was read verbatim from the official Parliament of Australia PDF; the AFP/ACT
arrangement from the AFP's own page; the prisoner statistic from the ABS release; the NT Police
from its own page. legislation.gov.au and some agency sites (CDPP) serve body text via a
single-page application or block automated fetching; those facts were obtained by search retrieval
of the official page and are stated as sourced, not quoted verbatim. Full detail:
[australia-source-register.md](./australia-source-register.md).
