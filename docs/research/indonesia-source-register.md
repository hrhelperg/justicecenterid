## Two currency points

1. **The corrections ministry changed in 2024** (Presidential Regulation 157/2024 created the
   Ministry of Immigration and Corrections out of the former Ministry of Law and Human Rights), so
   the World Prison Brief's own "Ministry of Justice and Human Rights" label is out of date; the
   current ministry is used.
2. **The World Prison Brief figures had updated** between the research pass (276,136 at 31 Dec 2025)
   and authoring (287,571 at 30 Jun 2026) — the current values were fetched by hand and are used.
   This is why the prison figure is re-confirmed by hand for every country.

## Deliberate limits

The exact allocation of investigative direction between the police and the prosecution under the new
2026 Criminal Procedure Code was not established from its operative articles and is not asserted; any
human-rights assessment of the new Criminal Code is left out as not primary-fetched.

## Model note

Indonesia is a unitary decentralised state — one country-level record, all functions national.
**Aceh** (Islamic criminal law via Sharia courts under the national judiciary) is a bounded
special-autonomy feature described in prose, not modelled as a separate jurisdiction record — the
same treatment given to Greece's Mount Athos.
