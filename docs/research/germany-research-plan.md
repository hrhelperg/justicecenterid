# Germany research plan

## Objective

Test whether the architecture can honestly represent a federal justice system. Publishing
Germany was the means; finding where the model breaks was the goal. Results are in
[germany-model-findings.md](./germany-model-findings.md).

## Why federalism was the target

France is unitary, so a single national description is accurate there. That meant the France
pilot exercised only half the jurisdiction model: it never created a `federal` record, never
needed to distinguish who legislates from who administers, and never had to refuse a
national-uniformity claim. Germany forces all three.

## Scope decision

Thirteen candidate modules. Eight published, five deferred. The gate, applied per module: **can
every institutional claim be traced to a source actually read, and can it be stated without
implying a national uniformity the sources do not support?**

The second half of that test is Germany-specific and is why oversight and history are deferred
despite being important — both would require a single national page describing arrangements that
differ by Land.

## Source hierarchy applied

Tier 1 throughout: the Basic Law, two GVG provisions, two StPO provisions, one Destatis
publication. Tier 2 for the one statistic: Council of Europe SPACE I. No journalism, no
secondary commentary, no Land sources.

## Method

1. Constitutional allocation first, because in a federal state it constrains everything below.
2. Code provisions read at section level in the **authoritative German**, not in translation.
3. Statistical sources read for methodology before figures — which is how the Destatis
   "transmitted by the Länder statistical offices" statement was found, and why no number is
   taken from Destatis at all.
4. Every URL opened and read. Nothing cited from a search snippet.

## Land sample selection

Three Länder, chosen for what each tests rather than for size or familiarity.

| Land                             | Tests                                                                     | Why it earns a record                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bavaria (`de-by`)                | A territorial Land owning court, police, prosecution and prison functions | The baseline federal case: a Land administering functions the Bund legislates on                                                                        |
| Berlin (`de-be`)                 | City-state                                                                | One body exercising Land **and** municipal levels at once, reusing `alsoExercisesLevels`. Makes "German municipal police" unstatable as a general claim |
| North Rhine-Westphalia (`de-nw`) | Intermediate tier                                                         | Maintains Regierungsbezirke, which the city-states lack entirely — the cleanest proof that the Länder are not one structure repeated sixteen times      |

No record exists for any Kreis, kreisfreie Stadt or municipality: those tiers are not uniform
across Länder, which is itself the finding, and tier records would assert a uniformity the
sources do not support.

**None of the three has any researched institutional detail.** They are structural samples and
assert nothing beyond what the Basic Law supports. No public Land page was created.

## What was researched but not published

| Module             | Blocker                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Forensics          | No source on institutional organisation. Distributed across federal and Land bodies, so an unsourced description would assert national uniformity in a safety-sensitive section.                                                                              |
| Border and customs | Only the Art. 87(1) constitutional basis read. Statutory competences of Bundespolizei, Zoll and Zollkriminalamt not established; the division between border policing, customs, immigration and tax enforcement is exactly what the module must not collapse. |
| Oversight          | Oversight is itself federally divided — federal and Land parliaments, federal and Land data-protection authorities, Land-specific complaint arrangements. A single national page would imply a uniform national complaints model that does not exist.         |
| History            | Spans six constitutional orders including the National Socialist dictatorship and the GDR. Requires archival and scholarly sources; claims of continuity across those ruptures require evidence, not inference.                                               |
| Timeline           | Only two dated facts established. Not a timeline.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

## Rules held throughout

- No institutional name translated into a foreign near-equivalent. Both analogies the brief
  names — "Germany's FBI", "Germany's national police" — appear **only** as criticised shorthand
  with the reason the equivalence fails.
- No claim about how any Land organises anything.
- No cross-country comparison, including with France. The one statistic carries its source's own
  warning against exactly that.
- Abbreviations expanded at first meaningful use: BKA, BPOL, BVerfG, BGH, GBA.
- No operational content: no deployment, tactics, surveillance capability, border procedure or
  investigative countermeasures.
- No legal advice.
