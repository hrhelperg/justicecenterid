# Denmark — source register

Every source used for the Denmark pages, verified on 2026-07-26 and independently re-checked (no
factual errors; three minor refinements applied — the ombudsman's constitutional section number is
not asserted, the Police Complaints Authority is dated 2010/2012, and police leadership is stated
only to what the fetched source supports).

## Access note

The Folketing's **official Constitution PDF** was behind a Cloudflare challenge and the
**domstol.dk** court pages were bot-walled, so the 1953 Constitution is cited from a reproduction
of the official English translation and the court hierarchy from the EU e-Justice Portal.

## Deliberate gaps (stated, not filled)

- Exact scope of the Minister of Justice's power to instruct prosecutors — not established.
- The Grundlov section number for the ombudsman (accessible rendering vs the widely-cited official
  number differ) — not asserted; the statutory basis (Ombudsman Act) is used.
- Faroese/Greenlandic justice devolution boundaries — noted as self-government, not modelled.
- Detailed composition/powers of the Judicial Appointments Council and the Special Court of
  Indictment and Revision; the internal structure of the Special Crime Unit.
