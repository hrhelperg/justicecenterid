# Sweden — research plan

## Jurisdiction scoping

Unitary constitutional monarchy with local self-government; not federal. All four justice functions
national, exercised by central-government administrative authorities. One country-level record, all
`own`.

## Research questions

- Constitutional framework (four fundamental laws; Instrument of Government).
- The ministerstyre prohibition (Ch. 12 Art. 2) — the constitutional insulation of agencies.
- Court structure: two branches (general + administrative), two apex courts; constitutional review
  (diffuse, Ch. 11 Art. 14; no constitutional court).
- Prosecution independence (case-insulated via the Constitution) and prosecutor-led investigation.
- Police (single national authority) and corrections (unified Kriminalvården).
- Oversight (JO under the Riksdag; JK under the Government).

## Model stress test

Reuse, no new schema. The ministerstyre prohibition is a strong agency-autonomy attribute, stated in
prose (not a new authority-basis value); the two-apex court structure and diffuse review are prose.
See sweden-model-findings.md.
