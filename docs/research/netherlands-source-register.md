# Netherlands — source register

Every source used for the Netherlands pages, verified on 2026-07-26 and independently re-checked
in an adversarial fact-check pass (all six load-bearing facts confirmed, no corrections).

## Access note

The official consolidated-legislation site **wetten.overheid.nl was unreachable** from the
research environment. The exact statutory text of the **Politiewet 2012** (Police Act 2012) and
the **Wet op de rechterlijke organisatie** (Judiciary (Organisation) Act — the OM, the Board of
Prosecutors General, and the Minister's power of instruction) is therefore **not quoted**; the
substance of each is confirmed from the official pages below. The Constitution is cited from the
government's official English translation (fetched as a PDF and text-extracted).
