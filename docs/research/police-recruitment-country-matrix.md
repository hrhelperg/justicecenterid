# Police recruitment country matrix

Six systems researched to content-confirmed Tier 1 sources on 6 September 2026. Status values:
**ESTABLISHED**, **PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED**, **VARIABLE WITHIN JURISDICTION**, **CAMPAIGN-SPECIFIC**,
**NOT ESTABLISHED**, **NOT RESEARCHED**. Absence of evidence is never recorded as "no requirement".

## 1. Identity and recruitment structure

| System          | Recruiting body                                                                                           | Structure                                   | Status                      |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Ireland         | An Garda Síochána; competition run by the Public Appointments Service on behalf of the Garda Commissioner | One national service, central competition   | ESTABLISHED                 |
| England & Wales | Individual forces, under national eligibility criteria published by the national recruitment service      | National criteria + local force criteria    | ESTABLISHED                 |
| New Zealand     | New Zealand Police                                                                                        | One national service, central recruitment   | ESTABLISHED                 |
| Netherlands     | Nationale Politie, through the Politieacademie                                                            | National, admission to education programmes | ESTABLISHED                 |
| Germany         | Each Land police force; Bundespolizei separately                                                          | No national recruitment                     | ESTABLISHED (as an absence) |
| United States   | Municipal, county, state and federal employers                                                            | No national recruitment                     | ESTABLISHED (as an absence) |

## 2. Status requirement — four different questions

| System          | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Status                          |

| New Zealand     | "a New Zealand or Australian citizen or have New Zealand residency or New Zealand permanent residency"                                                                                                                                                                     | ESTABLISHED                     |
| Netherlands     | Dutch nationality, which "mag ook je tweede nationaliteit zijn"                                                                                                                                                                                                            | ESTABLISHED                     |

| United States   | NOT ESTABLISHED — no national requirement; set per employer                                                                                                                                                                                                                | VARIABLE WITHIN JURISDICTION    |

**Only one of the four researched rules is a citizenship requirement.** Paraphrasing any of the
others as "must be a citizen" changes who is eligible.

## 3. Age

| System                 | Requirement                                                                                     | Status                                          |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| England & Wales        | 17 to apply; 18 to become an officer                                                            | ESTABLISHED                                     |
| New Zealand            | 17 to apply; 18 at the start of Police College; **"There's no upper age limit"**                | ESTABLISHED                                     |
| Netherlands            | 18 at the start of training. No maximum stated; none inferred                                   | PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED                           |
| Ireland                | "18 years of age but not yet 50 years of age **at midnight on Thursday, 8th of February 2024**" | **CAMPAIGN-SPECIFIC** — not published as a rule |
| Germany, United States | NOT RESEARCHED / NOT ESTABLISHED                                                                | —                                               |

Two systems separate an application age from an appointment age. One states affirmatively that no
maximum exists. One ties its window to a closing date. **No system supports a general statement
about police age limits.**

## 8. Entry-route structure — the finding Wave 24 established, confirmed here

| System               | Entry routes                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Netherlands          | Six: mbo-4 (2 yrs) plus four hbo bachelors (3 yrs) — Politiekunde Politieagent, Politiekunde **Wijkagent**, **Rechercheur**, Politieleider |
| Germany (Berlin)     | Two service tracks entered at different education levels                                                                                   |
| Ireland, New Zealand | One trainee route                                                                                                                          |
| England & Wales      | Multiple national entry programmes, plus force-level variation                                                                             |

Investigation and community policing are **entry routes** in the Netherlands, not later
assignments. The rank/role/specialisation distinction is not rewritten anywhere in this wave.

## 10. Deferred and not researched

**Czechia — DEFERRED.** `policie.cz` redirects via `policie.gov.cz` to **`archiv.policie.gov.cz`**,
which carries the notice "Nacházíte se na archivní verzi webu Policie České republiky. Obsah zde
nemusí být aktuální", is dated 17 May 2024, and does not contain the conditions — it points to
zákon č. 361/2003 Sb. §§ 13–16. Publishing requirements from a page that declares its own content
may be out of date is the failure this wave exists to prevent.

**Norway — attempted and abandoned.** `phs.no` 301-redirects to `politihogskolen.no`, whose
admission-requirements path then returns **HTTP 404**. Link rot, recorded.

**England & Wales — researched but no country module.** The corpus has no England-and-Wales dossier
to attach one to. The evidence powers the comparative guides instead. Recorded rather than worked
around by inventing a dossier.

**NOT RESEARCHED:** France (blocked in Wave 24, not retried), Poland, Spain, Canada, Australia.
None is described anywhere in this wave.
