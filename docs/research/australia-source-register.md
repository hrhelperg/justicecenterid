# Australia source register

**Research date:** confirmed **2026-07-26**. **Registry:** `src/content/sources.ts`, ids prefixed `au-`.

## Access standard

The Australian Constitution was read verbatim from the official Parliament of Australia
consolidated PDF; the AFP ACT-Policing page, the Northern Territory Police page, and the ABS
prisoner release were read directly. The Federal Register of Legislation (legislation.gov.au) and
some agency sites (the CDPP) serve their body text through a single-page application or block
automated fetching; facts resting on those pages were obtained by search retrieval of the official
page and are stated as sourced, with no verbatim quotation attributed to a page not read in full.

## Stated limitations

- **State/territory administration means aggregation.** The prisoner figure aggregates the
  separately run state and territory systems; there is no Commonwealth prison system.
- **SPA/blocked sources are sourced, not quoted.** Where a fact rests on legislation.gov.au or the
  CDPP site, it is stated as sourced from the official page, not as a verbatim quotation.
- **No individual state/territory or Indigenous jurisdiction researched.** The samples carry the
  constitutional and arrangement structure only.
