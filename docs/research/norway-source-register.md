# Norway — source register

Every source used for the Norway pages, verified on 2026-07-26 and independently re-checked (no
factual errors; the verification pass upgraded two flags — 12 police districts is firmly confirmed,
and the prosecution's statutory basis is the Criminal Procedure Act).

## Access note

Several official sites (politiet.no and kriminalomsorgen.no) are client-side rendered and returned
no directly-fetched paragraph, so the police-district count rests on official descriptions plus the
Storting reform page, and the correctional-service ministry is corroborated by the World Prison
Brief. The Constitution was read from Lovdata (official).
