# Equipment: commercial opportunity map

**INTERNAL STRATEGY DOCUMENT. NOT PUBLISHED.**
`docs/` is never routed and never exported — no page imports it, `verify-output.mjs` does not see
it, and nothing under `out/` contains it. Verified for this wave.

This document evaluates equipment categories for _future_ commerce optionality. **No commerce is
implemented, and none may be implemented from this document alone.** It exists so that a later
decision is made against a written classification rather than against whatever seems convenient at
the time.

---

## The rule this whole document exists to protect

> An editorial entity page must never silently become a commercial page.

"What is a police flashlight?" answers a question. "Best police flashlights to buy" sells a
flashlight. If those are ever the same URL, the corpus has traded its authority for a click, and
every other page loses credibility with it — because a reader who finds one commercial page has no
way to know which of the other 550 are.

**Design consequence:** any future commercial surface must be a _separate route_ with _separate
authorship_, linking to the editorial page rather than replacing it. The editorial page must remain
complete and useful with the commercial surface deleted.

## Three-tier classification

### Tier A — future commerce-compatible

Ordinary professional goods, sold to anyone, with no restricted status and no operational risk.

| Category                               | Educational value                | Search intent | Commerce fit | Regulatory risk | Verdict    |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------ | --------------- | ---------- |
| Notebooks and documentation stationery | High — already an editorial page | Mixed         | High         | None            | **HIGH**   |
| Duty bags, backpacks, organisers       | Medium                           | Commercial    | High         | None            | **MEDIUM** |
| Footwear                               | Medium                           | Commercial    | High         | None            | **MEDIUM** |
| General flashlights                    | Medium                           | Commercial    | High         | Low             | **MEDIUM** |
| Watches                                | Low                              | Commercial    | High         | None            | **LOW**    |
| General apparel and base layers        | Low                              | Commercial    | Medium       | None            | **LOW**    |
| Books and study material               | High                             | Informational | High         | None            | **HIGH**   |
| General fitness accessories            | Low                              | Commercial    | Medium       | None            | **LOW**    |

Highest genuine fit: **books and documentation stationery**, because the editorial page already
exists, the audience is real, and neither carries any restricted-goods question.

### Tier B — editorial and institutional only

Publishable as knowledge; never a commerce target, because the buyer is an institution and the
retail version of the question is a different, worse question.

- Body-worn cameras
- Police radios and communications
- Body armour
- Identification systems and warrant cards
- Vehicle electronics and emergency lighting
- Equipment standards and testing regimes

These already have editorial coverage or are legitimate future editorial subjects. A commercial
surface on any of them would put this platform in the position of appearing to supply police
equipment, which is a category error about what this site is.

### Tier C — prohibited

Never a commerce target, and editorial coverage only at institutional level.

Firearms · ammunition · firearm accessories · batons · restraints · conducted energy devices ·
irritant sprays · weapon systems · breaching tools · covert surveillance equipment · interception
equipment · counter-surveillance equipment

**Verdict: INAPPROPRIATE**, without exception and without a review path in this programme.

## Where the durable commercial value actually sits

Not in equipment pages. The audit points elsewhere:

1. **Training institutions** (Wave 35) — a verified directory has real user value, natural
   commercial adjacency, and no restricted-goods problem.
2. **Careers and recruitment** (Waves 24–28) — the strongest existing asset, and the audience most
   likely to buy anything.
3. **Books and study material** — the one Tier A category with high educational value already.

Equipment is a good _knowledge_ subject and a poor _commerce_ subject for this platform, because
the parts people want to buy are the parts we should not rank, and the parts we can describe
honestly are institutional.

---

## Standing constraints on any future implementation

1. Paid placement must be labelled.
2. Sponsorship must not alter a factual conclusion.
3. Affiliate revenue must not determine ordering.
4. Official sources remain authoritative over any commercial claim.
5. No manufacturer may purchase editorial status.
6. No "best" or ranking page without an explicitly approved, published methodology.
7. Every knowledge page must remain useful and complete with all commerce removed.
8. Tier C is closed permanently.
