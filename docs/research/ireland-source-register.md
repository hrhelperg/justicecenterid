# Ireland source register

**Research date:** all sources read or retrieved and confirmed **2026-07-25**.
**Registry:** `src/content/sources.ts`, ids prefixed `ie-`, plus `coe-space-i-2024`.

## Access standard, stated plainly

Irish official legal and government sites (irishstatutebook.ie, gov.ie,
citizensinformation.ie, courts.ie, garda.ie) systematically return **HTTP 403** to automated
requests behind a web-application firewall, while serving normally to a browser. This is the
same class of block documented for legifrance.gouv.fr (France) and justice.gov (US), but far
more pervasive across the Irish government estate.

The master specification is explicit: _"A script-blocked official legal source must not be
discarded merely because automated fetching fails … if it can be independently verified through
a reader-accessible official document."_ Applied here:

| Access path                                                                                                                                    | Sources                                                                                                      | What it means                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Direct read** (WebFetch reached the page)                                                                                                    | `ie-dpp` (dppireland.ie), `ie-fiosru` (fiosru.ie); `coe-space-i-2024` extracted directly from the report PDF | Strongest. Verbatim quotes are taken only from these.                                                           |
| **Search retrieval of the exact official page** (WAF-blocked to direct fetch; content obtained via search of that page and cross-corroborated) | `ie-courts-service`, `ie-garda-act-2005`, `ie-gov-pscsa-2024`, `ie-citizensinfo-scc`, `ie-citizensinfo-gfa`  | The reader-accessible official document behind the block. Stated as sourced facts, never as verbatim quotation. |

No verbatim quotation is attributed to a source that was not read in full. No constitutional
article is quoted verbatim, because the primary Constitution text (irishstatutebook.ie /
gov.ie) was WAF-blocked; the constitutional provisions are described through the official
Courts Service and Citizens Information material and cross-corroborated.

## Outstanding

- No verbatim constitutional text; provisions are described via official secondary sources.
- The powers, procedures and findings of Fiosrú and the Policing and Community Safety Authority
  are not researched beyond their identity, basis and commencement date.
- The internal structure of An Garda Síochána, the courts' detailed jurisdiction, and the Irish
  Prison Service's individual prisons are not researched.
