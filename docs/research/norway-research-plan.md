# Norway — research plan

## Jurisdiction scoping

Unitary, decentralised (non-federal) constitutional monarchy; non-EU (EEA/Schengen). All four
justice functions national, under the Ministry of Justice and Public Security. One country-level
record, all `own`. No sub-national justice tier.

## Research questions

- Constitutional basis (1814 Grunnloven): courts, judicial independence, constitutional review.
- Is there a constitutional court? (No — ordinary courts review, Art. 89.)
- Prosecution independence: how strong, and by what mechanism? (Only the King in Council may
  instruct, not the Minister; DPP appointed by King in Council.)
- Prosecutor-led, police-integrated investigation (lowest prosecution tier inside the police).
- Police structure and the 2016 proximity-police reform (27 → 12 districts).
- Prison authority (Kriminalomsorgen, Ministry of Justice and Public Security).
- Oversight (Parliamentary Ombud; Bureau for the Investigation of Police Affairs; Courts Administration).

## Model stress test

Reuse, no new schema. The prosecutor-led police-integrated investigation and the strong
prosecutorial independence are functional/attribute facts in prose, not new levels or scopes.
See norway-model-findings.md.

## Source hierarchy

→ Storting (the 2016 reform) → the ombud and police-affairs bureau → World Prison Brief (ministry)
→ Council of Europe SPACE I (prison figures).
