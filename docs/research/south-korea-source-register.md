## Access notes and honest limits

- The Constitution is an **unofficial** translation (Constitute); the Korean text is authoritative.
- The **Korean National Police Agency**'s ministry placement and the **2020–2021 investigation-power
  rebalance** rest on reform reporting and secondary reading (the official police organic law was
  not fetched); the **National Human Rights Commission**, **Anti-Corruption and Civil Rights
  Commission**, and **Corruption Investigation Office** are named from secondary/tertiary reading.
  These limits are stated on the pages.
- The **2025 restructuring** is attributed to **Korea Herald** because the primary gazette and the
  implementing statutes (a Serious Crime Investigation Agency Act and an Indictment Office Act) were
  not fetchable — the Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor page was bot-walled.
- The prosecution's placement **under the Ministry of Justice** is attributed to the Government
  Organization Act / Prosecutors' Office Act, not to the prosecution's own page (which does not
  state it).

## The scheduled change

The enacted 2025 restructuring is also recorded as a structured `ScheduledChange`
(`kr-prosecution-restructuring-2026`): `certainty: 'enacted-with-date'`, `status: 'pending'`,
`effectiveOn: '2026-10-02'`. It is the first non-European scheduled change on the site and the first
genuine `pending` one since the foundation pilots — the Italy separation-of-careers and Austria
Bundesstaatsanwaltschaft reforms were only proposals and were kept in prose, not the scheduled-change
registry.
