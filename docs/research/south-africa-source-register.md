## The federalism finding (the data-model driver)

Verbatim from the schedules: courts, prosecution, prisons and criminal law appear in **neither**
Schedule 4 (concurrent) **nor** Schedule 5 (exclusive provincial), so they fall to the national
sphere; the only provincial justice-adjacent competence is police "to the extent that Chapter 11
confers", which s 206(3) limits to **oversight**, not command. So despite nine provinces, all four
functions are national — modelled as **one country record, no sub-national record** (the opposite of
Mexico, Argentina and India, which got sub-national records).

## Corrections applied from verification

- An **unattested nickname** for the current National Director of Public Prosecutions was dropped
  (the official appointment source does not carry it).
- The **NPA Amendment Act signing (24 May 2024) and gazette (27 May 2024)** dates are not conflated;
  the commencement (19 Aug 2024) is used.

## Deliberate omissions

The **2025–2026 judicial commission of inquiry** into alleged police interference and the leadership
suspensions around it are left out — unresolved personnel and inquiry matters that do not change the
institutional structure. An **unverified IPID Amendment Bill assent** is not asserted. Whether IDAC
is sufficiently independent is a **contested** question, recorded by attribution.
