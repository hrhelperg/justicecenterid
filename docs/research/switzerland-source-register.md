# Switzerland source register

**Research date:** confirmed **2026-07-26**. **Registry:** `src/content/sources.ts`, ids prefixed `ch-`.

## Multilingual authority

Swiss federal law is enacted and equally authentic in German, French and Italian (Constitution
art. 70); Romansh is a national language, official when dealing with Romansh speakers. The English
texts on fedlex are non-authoritative reference translations. These pages cite the English for
readability, keep the original-language institution names, and note that the English has no legal
force.

## Access standard

The Federal Constitution and the Criminal Procedure Code were read from the official fedlex texts
(the fedlex ELI HTML pages require JavaScript, so the paired data-store text was used); the
detainee figure from the FSO release; the fedpol and BEKJ facts from the agencies' own pages,
obtained by search retrieval where the site serves a single-page application. No verbatim quotation
is attributed to a page not read in full.

## Stated limitations

- **The English is a translation.** Any legal point rests on the German, French or Italian text.
- **The detainee figure is a national aggregate** of the cantonal systems (no federal prison
  system) on a single reference day.
- **No individual canton or concordat researched** beyond the constitutional structure; the
  concordat memberships are stated from the constitutional basis, not researched institution by
  institution.
