# Portugal — source register

Research date: **2026-07-26**. Every source below was read or retrieved on that date, confirmed to
contain the facts attributed to it, and independently re-checked in an adversarial verification pass.
This register records each source's exact scope and where its scope ends, so nothing on the Portugal
pages rests on more than its source will bear.

**Scope note — Portugal, not Brazil.** Portugal and Brazil share the Portuguese language and some
institution names (both call their prosecution service the _Ministério Público_). They are different
states with different constitutions. Every source here is Portuguese, and the pages describe only
Portugal.

## Access notes

- **The official consolidated-legislation portal (`diariodarepublica.pt`) is a client-side
  application** that did not serve statute text to an automated request. The LOIC and the Code of
  Criminal Procedure were therefore read from official-origin PDFs (a Diário da República version of
  the LOIC hosted on the police's own site; a consolidated Code of Criminal Procedure PDF), each
  text-extracted and confirmed to contain the cited articles.
- **The Constitution** was read from the Assembly of the Republic's own official English translation
  (the body that enacts it), with the Portuguese text cross-read.

## Correction applied from verification

The adversarial verification pass flagged that the autonomy of the Public Prosecution Service must
**not** be attributed to the EU e-Justice page, which carries no prosecution content. The autonomy
is therefore anchored to **Constitution Article 219(2)** ("its own statute and autonomy"), and the
e-Justice page is cited only for the court hierarchy. This correction is reflected in
`prosecution`, the source records, and the sources module.

## Deferrals

`forensics`, `border-and-customs`, `history` and `timeline` are **not** published. Forensics and
border/customs are safety-sensitive and were not researched to standard; history and timeline
require scholarly and per-milestone primary sources not assembled here. Each carries a stated
`deferredReason` in the dossier and produces no route.
