# Finland — source register

Every source used for the Finland pages, verified on 2026-07-26 and independently re-checked (no
factual errors).

## Access note

The Constitution and the Åland Autonomy Act are the Ministry of Justice's official English
translations, fetched as PDFs and text-extracted (finlex.fi's own pages are client-side rendered).
The Åland source establishes the competence allocation only, not the administration of a distinct
Åland police organisation.

## Deliberate gaps (stated, not filled)

- The name/administration of a distinct Åland police organisation — only the competence is sourced.
- A dedicated standalone police-complaints body (complaints fall to the two guardians of legality).
- Any separate judicial-appointments/discipline council beyond the National Courts Administration and ss. 102/110.
- Any proposed/scheduled future reform.
