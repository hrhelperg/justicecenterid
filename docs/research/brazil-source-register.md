# Brazil source register

**Research date:** all sources read or extracted and confirmed **2026-07-25**.
**Registry:** `src/content/sources.ts`, ids prefixed `br-`.

## Access standard, stated plainly

The Presidência da República site (planalto.gov.br), portal.stf.jus.br and cnj.jus.br **reset or
refuse** an ordinary programmatic request, but serve the full text to a **browser user-agent**.
This is the same class of block documented for legifrance.gouv.fr (France) and the Japanese
official sites (Japan), and the master specification permits such a source to be used where it
can be read through a reader-accessible path. Applied here:

| Access path                                                             | Sources                                                                                                                                 | What it means                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Direct read via browser user-agent** (full page retrieved and parsed) | `br-cf-1988` (Constitution), `br-cpp-1941` (Code of Criminal Procedure), `br-lep-1984` (Lei de Execução Penal) — all planalto.gov.br    | Strongest. Every constitutional/statutory quotation on the pages was extracted verbatim from these full reads. |
| **Direct read** (institution's own page)                                | `br-pf-competencias`, `br-prf-competencias` (gov.br), `br-mpu-institucional` (mpu.mp.br), `br-senappen-institucional` (gov.br/senappen) | Read in full; the institutions' own statements of their remit.                                                 |
| **Direct PDF extraction** (primary report)                              | `br-sisdepen-2s2024` (SENAPPEN SISDEPEN PDF)                                                                                            | The prison statistic, extracted from the report's totals pages.                                                |

No verbatim quotation is attributed to a source that was not read in full.

## Translation status

Brazilian law is authoritative only in **Portuguese**, and the Presidência da República hosts
**no official English translation**. Every source below is therefore cited in its authoritative
Portuguese; because the sources are the originals rather than translations, `translationStatus`
is left at its default and the translation-integrity point is carried in prose (a "Reading the
law in Portuguese" callout on the hub and the justice-system and sources modules). The higher
courts (STF, STJ) do publish official English descriptions of themselves, but with the standard
caveat that the Portuguese prevails — so no English text is authoritative for legal citation.

## Stated limitations

- **The English is descriptive.** All constitutional and statutory text is cited from the
  authoritative Portuguese; the English on the pages renders what a provision does, not its
  binding wording.
- **The prison figure is one measure among several.** The 670,265 "cela física" total excludes
  the large home-detention population the same report tabulates separately; it is a single-day
  snapshot aggregating 27 systems, and the report prints no occupancy rate.
- **No individual state institution was researched.** São Paulo and the Federal District exist in
  the jurisdiction model as constitutional-structure samples, not as researched institutions.
