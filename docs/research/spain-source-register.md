# Spain source register

**Research date:** confirmed **2026-07-26**. **Registry:** `src/content/sources.ts`, ids prefixed `es-`.

## Language

State law is authoritative in Castilian Spanish (boe.es) and is cited in Castilian. Catalan, Basque
and Galician are co-official in their communities, and the community institutions are named in
those languages (the Mossos d'Esquadra in Catalan, the Ertzaintza in Basque) with no invented
English name.

## Access standard

The Constitution, the security-forces organic law and the two prison-transfer decrees were read
verbatim from the Boletín Oficial del Estado (boe.es). The Spanish statistical portals
(interior.gob.es, institucionpenitenciaria.es) block automated fetching; because the prison
figures also carry a scope trap (the central series excludes Catalonia and the Basque Country), no
prison figure is published (see the corrections page). No verbatim quotation is attributed to a
source not read in full.

## Stated limitations

- **Administration devolved, legislation national.** The transfers devolve the execution of State
  penitentiary legislation; the penitentiary law itself stays the State's (art. 149.1.6).
- **No prison figure.** The official series are split by administration and could not be reconciled
  to one verified, properly scoped number; none is published.
- **No individual community researched** beyond the constitutional and transfer structure; the
  autonomous-police naming rests on the LO 2/1986 framework and art. 149.1.29.
