# Singapore — research plan

Singapore is country 3 of Batch C. It is unitary (low jurisdiction-model pressure) but adds a
genuinely new prosecution shape and a rigorous test of the site's honesty rule about recording
absences.

## Questions the pilot had to answer

1. **Federal or unitary?** — a unitary city-state; judicial power in a single Supreme Court
   (Art. 93), one Legislature (Art. 38), no distribution-of-powers lists. One `sg` record, all
   functions `own`, no sub-national record.
2. **Where does the prosecution sit?** — the Attorney-General is BOTH the Government's legal adviser
   (Art. 35(7)) and the Public Prosecutor (Art. 35(8); Criminal Procedure Code s 11) — a fusion of
   adviser and prosecutor in one constitutional officer, not a Cabinet minister and removable only
   via a judicial tribunal.
3. **What is the apex court and the review model?** — the Court of Appeal is the final court; the
   High Court has an Appellate Division and a General Division (2019/2021 restructuring). Review is
   diffuse, with no dedicated constitutional court.
4. **Who investigates?** — the police (and specialist agencies) investigate; the Public Prosecutor
   evaluates the evidence and decides charges. Common-law police-led, like New Zealand.
5. **Police, prisons, oversight?** — the Singapore Police Force and the Singapore Prison Service are
   both under the Ministry of Home Affairs. Oversight is defined partly by absence — no general
   ombudsman, no national human-rights institution, no independent police-complaints body — with the
   CPIB (in the Prime Minister's Office) for corruption and the Presidential Council for Minority
   Rights for legislative scrutiny.
6. **Prisons and numbers?** — World Prison Brief: 10,792 at 31 Dec 2024, rate 178; capacity is 2013,
   so no current occupancy is claimed.

## The honesty test: recording absences

Singapore is the batch's clearest case of the site's presence-state rule ("absent, recorded
explicitly, because a blank would be indistinguishable from unresearched"). The oversight page states
the three missing institutions as researched negatives rather than leaving a gap — and excludes the
contested 2026 UPR debate over establishing an NHRI, because the Government's formal response could
not be independently confirmed.

## Sources targeted

The Constitution and Criminal Procedure Code (Singapore Statutes Online), the Attorney-General's
Chambers, the Judiciary, the Singapore Police Force, and the World Prison Brief — all in English.

## Model discipline

No new jurisdiction schema. The adviser/prosecutor fusion and the recorded absences live in prose
and in the tests.
