# Wave 11 — comparative defence and legal-representation matrix

Rows are systems. Columns are structural questions. Values are `YES`, `NO`, `CONDITIONAL`,
`PARTIAL` and `NOT ESTABLISHED`.

`NOT ESTABLISHED` is never rendered as `NO`. Only three systems were reached from primary sources
for this wave; the rest are absent, and that absence is a gap in the research rather than a
finding about those countries.

## B. When representation is required, and what triggers it

| System      | Trigger                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Means-tested?                                                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Germany** | **Necessity, not means.** StPO § 140 lists the situations of _notwendige Verteidigung_: first-instance trial before the higher regional court, regional court or Schöffengericht; a Verbrechen charged; proceedings that may lead to a professional ban; being brought before a court on detention or provisional placement; being held in an institution by judicial order; a psychiatric placement assessment; and further defined cases | **NO.** The trigger is the seriousness of the matter and the accused's situation |
| **Brazil**  | Constitutional entitlement to state assistance for "os que comprovarem insuficiência de recursos" (Art. 5º LXXIV)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | **YES**                                                                          |
| **France**  | _Aide juridictionnelle_ on three conditions, including that resources not exceed admission ceilings                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | **YES**, and graduated — the State covers "tout ou partie"                       |

**This column alone disposes of the assumption that publicly funded defence is everywhere a
poverty programme.** Germany's mandatory-defence trigger asks what the case is, not what the
accused earns.

## C. Who the lawyer is, and who employs them

| System      | The lawyer                                                         | Employed by the state?                                                                                                                                                                                 | Appointed by                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Germany** | A _Pflichtverteidiger_ — a Rechtsanwalt appointed under StPO § 141 | **NO**                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Appointed in the cases of necessary defence, without delay on request after instruction, and independently of any request in defined situations including detention |
| **France**  | An _avocat_, whose fees the State covers in whole or in part       | **NO**                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Aid granted on application; the residence condition does not apply to persons involved in criminal proceedings                                                      |
| **Brazil**  | A _Defensor Público_ — a career officer of the Defensoria Pública  | **This is the exception.** Career posts filled at the initial class by public competition (concurso público de provas e títulos), with _inamovibilidade_ guaranteed and **private practice forbidden** | Institutional, not case-by-case appointment                                                                                                                         |

**Appointment and employment are different facts, and only Brazil couples them.** In Germany and
France the lawyer the State pays for is a private practitioner.

## G. What the matrix forces

**1. "Right to counsel" is three different rights, and systems grant them separately.**
The right to consult a lawyer, the right to have one appointed, and the right to have the State
pay are distinct. Germany's § 137 grants the first at any stage to everyone; § 140 grants the
second on necessity; neither is conditioned on means, while Brazil's and France's funding
entitlements are. A sentence saying "everyone has the right to a free lawyer" is false about all
three systems, in three different ways.

**2. Publicly funded does not mean publicly employed.**
Germany appoints a private Rechtsanwalt. France pays a private avocat. Only Brazil employs its
defenders, and it does so through a constitutional career service whose members may not practise
privately. Court appointment, state funding and state employment are three separate facts.

**3. Legal aid and public defence are not synonyms.**
France operates a means-tested funding scheme with no defence institution at all. Brazil operates
a constitutional institution whose eligibility is means-based. Germany operates a
necessity-triggered appointment scheme that is neither. Using one country's word for another
country's arrangement misdescribes it.

**4. The Defensoria Pública is not a public defender office.**
Article 134 gives it the constitutional standing of the Ministério Público, a human-rights
promotion mandate, standing for collective and extrajudicial matters, irremovable members and a
bar on private practice. A public defender office provides criminal defence to indigent
defendants. The shared function conceals a difference in kind, and this wave publishes no defence
institution family because of it.

**5. Defence rights are bounded in the instruments that grant them.**
Germany's § 147 grants file access and then limits it; § 148 grants communication and then carves
a narrow exception. Neither right is absolute, and both limits are written into the same section
as the right. A page claiming absolute privilege or unlimited access would contradict the statute
it cites.
