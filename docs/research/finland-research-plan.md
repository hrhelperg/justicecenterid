# Finland — research plan

## Jurisdiction scoping

Unitary, bilingual (Finnish/Swedish), EU parliamentary republic. On the mainland all four justice
functions are national. ONE asymmetry: the autonomous region of Åland holds legislative competence
over policing (Autonomy Act s. 18(6)) while courts, prosecution and corrections stay with the State
(s. 27). Two jurisdiction records: `fi` (country, all own) and `fi-aland` (autonomous-community,
policing own, the rest national).

## Research questions

- Constitutional framework (1999): twin apex courts (s. 3/98/99), no constitutional court (s. 106),
  bilingualism (s. 17), EU membership (s. 1).
- Two guardians of legality (Chancellor of Justice s. 108; Parliamentary Ombudsman s. 109).
- Prosecution independence (decisional; within the MoJ administrative branch; s. 104, Act 32/2019).
- Police under the Ministry of the Interior (a different ministry from the other justice bodies).
- Investigation direction (police-led; prosecutor-led exception for police-suspect cases).
- Corrections (Rikosseuraamuslaitos, under the MoJ).
- The Åland competence split.

## Model stress test

Reuse — but Finland is the batch's real pressure test. The Åland asymmetry (one autonomous region
running its own policing over a national baseline) is modelled by REUSING the `autonomous-community`
level minted by the Spain pilot, validating it in a second country. See finland-model-findings.md.
