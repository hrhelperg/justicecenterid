# New Zealand — research plan

New Zealand is country 1 of Batch C (a global-diversity batch of ten). It is the batch's
lowest-model-pressure country — unitary, single national bodies — but it adds three things the site
did not yet have: an uncodified constitution under parliamentary sovereignty, a common-law
Law-Officer prosecution, and a recognised indigenous source of law.

## Questions the pilot had to answer

1. **Federal or unitary?** — unitary; no state/provincial or local justice competence. One `nz`
   record, all functions `own`, no sub-national record.
2. **What is the constitutional framework?** — uncodified: the Constitution Act 1986 + convention +
   common law + the Treaty of Waitangi. Parliament is sovereign; the courts cannot strike down
   statutes. Under the NZ Bill of Rights Act 1990 they may issue declarations of inconsistency.
3. **Is there an indigenous-law dimension, and how is it stated?** — yes: tikanga Māori, recognised
   by the Supreme Court in Ellis v R [2022] NZSC 114 as "the first law of Aotearoa New Zealand".
   Stated strictly by attribution to the judgment, not decoratively.
4. **What is the apex court?** — the Supreme Court of New Zealand (2004, replaced Privy Council
   appeals); diffuse review, no strike-down; no dedicated constitutional court.
5. **Where does prosecution sit?** — a Law-Officer model: the Attorney-General + Solicitor-General
   (Crown Law) superintend, Crown Solicitors conduct serious cases, the Police prosecute lesser
   matters. No standalone Director of Public Prosecutions.
6. **Who investigates?** — the Police, independently of ministers (Policing Act 2008 s 16(2)); the
   common-law police-led model, the clean opposite of the civil-law prosecutor-led model in Batch B.
7. **Prisons and numbers?** — the Department of Corrections (Ara Poutama Aotearoa). World Prison
   Brief: 11,255 at 31 Mar 2026, rate 211/100k; capacity is 2019, so no current occupancy claimed.

## Sources targeted

Constitution Act 1986 and Policing Act 2008 (via mirrors — official portal WAF-walled); the
judiciary's own site and the Ministry of Justice; the Crown Law Office; the IPCA; Ellis v R; and the
World Prison Brief. Non-European, so the Council of Europe SPACE I dataset does not apply — World
Prison Brief is the prison-statistics source for this and every Batch C country.

## What was deliberately left out

Sentencing-policy changes (the 2024 three-strikes and gang laws) are not institutional-structure
facts and are not described. Forensics, border/customs, history and a timeline are deferred with
reasons and produce no routes.

## Model discipline

No new schema. New Zealand's distinctiveness — uncodified constitution, tikanga, Law-Officer
prosecution, police-led investigation — lives entirely in prose and in the tests.
