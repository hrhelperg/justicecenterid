# Wave 9 — cannibalization analysis

Twenty-eight guide candidates and ten institution candidates were assessed. **Ten routes were
published** — nine guides and one institution type.

Comparison set: `/courts/what-do-courts-do`, the seven court-related glossary terms,
`/professions/judge`, the six `/investigations/*` guides, `/prosecution/what-does-a-prosecutor-do`,
`/justice/what-is-due-process`, `/justice/what-is-the-rule-of-law`, the seventeen
`/law-enforcement/*` guides, the 32 country `courts` modules, and the institution registry.

## The constraint that shaped the wave

Seven Part B candidates are already owned by the glossary — _court_, _judicial independence_,
_judicial review_, _appeal_, _jurisdiction_, _due process_, _rule of law_. `/professions/judge`
owns the judicial role and already asks how a judge can be accountable and independent at once.
`what-do-courts-do` owns the function question.

The space left is **structural and comparative**. Every published page owns a question about how
court systems are _organised_ and how they _differ_ — not what a term means.

## Institution candidates

| Candidate                                 | Decision                     | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Constitutional court**                  | **PUBLISH**                  | The only court type with genuine structural recurrence: Germany, Spain and Belgium each maintain a body **separate from the ordinary judiciary** with authority over legislation. Counterexamples are carried on the page — Brazil and Japan merge the function into the apex court, Ireland disperses it to the High Court, and the Netherlands forbids it under Art. 120 |
| Trial court                               | **DEFER**                    | A _position_ in a hierarchy, not an institution family. Published as a guide instead (#6)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Appellate court                           | **DEFER**                    | Same                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Supreme court                             | **REJECT as an institution** | The evidence forbids it. The label covers a final appellate court, a constitutional court, both at once, and one apex among several. Published as a guide about that variation                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Court of cassation                        | **REJECT as an institution** | Sourced in one jurisdiction here. Cassation is a _mode of review_, covered in #17                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Administrative court                      | **DEFER**                    | Recurs, but as a _jurisdictional order_ rather than a body type. Published as a guide                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Specialized court                         | **REJECT**                   | A residual category, not a family                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Federal court / State court / Local court | **REJECT**                   | Positions within a federal arrangement, already covered by the jurisdiction cluster                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

**One institution route.** The discipline is Wave 7's: a global institution family must recur
structurally, and a shared name is not recurrence.

## Boundary compliance

**Prosecution (Wave 10).** No page discusses charging standards, prosecutorial discretion, plea
bargaining or prosecution independence. Prosecutors appear only as one of three functions in the
adjudicative model. Asserted by test.

**Defence.** Rights are acknowledged — counsel, challenging evidence, hearing rights, appeal
rights — because they explain why courts matter. No page explains how defence works. Asserted by
test.

**Investigations (Wave 8).** Courts material stays in `/courts`; the Wave 8 boundary test, which
forbids court hierarchy and appellate structure on investigations pages, keeps passing.

**Professions.** No page defines the judicial role, judicial qualifications or judicial careers.
Asserted by test.

**No redirects.** No existing URL was renamed, removed or re-parented. Ten URLs were added.
