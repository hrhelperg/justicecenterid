## The one reversal, and why it is not convenience

Wave 5 deferred `internal-vs-external-police-oversight` as a restatement of
`how-police-are-held-to-account`. On Wave 5's evidence that was right: a page about the
internal/external split would have added a heading and no fact.

Wave 7 reverses it because two statutory clauses reached in this wave make the page's thesis a
finding rather than a framing:

- Sweden's police authority describes its investigating department as **"en oberoende avdelning
  inom Polismyndigheten, avskild från övrig polisverksamhet"** — an independent department
  _inside_ the Police Authority.
- Section 87 of Kenya's National Police Service Act provides that its Internal Affairs Unit
  **"shall not be subject to the control, direction or command of the Kenya Police,
  Administration Police or the Directorate"**.

Neither proposition appears anywhere in `how-police-are-held-to-account`, whose "Our reading"
callout argues that powers matter as much as independence — a different point. The reversal is
asserted by a test, so the guide cannot be unpublished without someone reading why it was
published.

## Intent separation between the two new guides

|                  | `internal-vs-external-police-oversight`                     | `police-complaints-vs-criminal-investigation`                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Question         | Where does the body sit, and does that make it independent? | What kind of process is this, and which stage is the body in?              |
| Axis             | Position                                                    | Sequence                                                                   |
| Payload          | Position does not predict independence, in either direction | The five stages are separable, and some bodies hold one without the others |
| Primary evidence | Sweden SU, Kenya IAU s. 87, France IGPN/DDD                 | Norway § 34-5, Czech Act, Ireland §§ 200–202, South Africa § 30            |

A test asserts their questions differ and that their key-term sets are not identical.

## Effect on existing pages

`independent-police-complaints-body` was **corrected**, not merely cross-linked. Wave 5 listed
Norway and Czechia as country examples of a complaints body; neither takes complaints. Both notes
were rewritten to present them as limits on the page and to point at the new family. This removes
a false equivalence rather than creating a competing page.

`how-police-are-held-to-account` **absorbed** the `who-disciplines-police` candidate.

**No redirects.** No existing URL was renamed, removed or re-parented. Three URLs were added.
