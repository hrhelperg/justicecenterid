## Verified distinguishing facts

- Regionalised state, but justice fully national: the five special-autonomy regions hold no justice
  competence (Art. 117) — the opposite of Finland's Åland.
- Prosecutors are magistrates within the independent judiciary; mandatory prosecution (Art. 112).
- Three top courts (Cassation, Constitutional Court, Council of State) — with a dedicated constitutional court.
- Multiple national police forces (Polizia di Stato, Carabinieri, Guardia di Finanza); local-police carve-out.
- Prisons: SPACE I 2024 density 118.1 (highest in the batch, well above capacity).
