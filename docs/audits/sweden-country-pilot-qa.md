## Verified distinguishing facts

- Ministerstyre prohibition (Instrument of Government Ch. 12 Art. 2): no minister may direct how the
  police, prosecution or prison service decides an individual case — constitutional agency autonomy.
- Two branches of courts, two apex courts (Supreme Court and Supreme Administrative Court, Ch. 11 Art. 1);
  no constitutional court, diffuse review (Ch. 11 Art. 14).
- Prosecutor-led preliminary investigation; unified corrections (Kriminalvården: prisons + remand +
  probation + transport).
- Oversight split by the Constitution: JO under the Riksdag, JK under the Government.
- Prisons: SPACE I 2024 density 104.9 (above capacity).
